Privacy Policy - Leehu Auto Publisher

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Leehu Auto Publisher is an internal tool used by Leehu Tsuri's social media agency to publish content and reply to comments on Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages that the agency manages on behalf of its clients, with their permission.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses the managed Page and Instagram account, their posts, comments and messages, only in order to publish content and respond to the audience on the owner's behalf.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to operate the managed accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored securely on the agency's own computer.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, or to revoke access, email leehu.tsuri@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". You can also remove the app at any time from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites. Requests are handled within 30 days.
